Sales Margin & Quotation Approval

Community Enterprise Odoo.sh

Sales Approval Matrix is an Odoo add-on for the Sales module that automates the quotation approval process based on configurable revenue and margin criteria. Instead of relying on manual checks, the module evaluates each quotation's untaxed revenue and overall margin percentage against a configurable approval matrix. Based on the matching rule, the quotation is classified into one of three approval zones—Green, Yellow, or Red. The assigned zone is clearly displayed on the quotation form using color-coded banners and highlighted order lines, allowing users to quickly identify the approval status.

When a quotation falls into the Yellow or Red approval zone, the module enforces an approval workflow before the quotation can proceed. The salesperson must submit an approval request, after which a Sales Manager is notified through activities and chatter messages. The manager can review the quotation and either approve or reject the request, with the decision and reason recorded in the quotation's history for complete traceability.

Until the required approval is granted, the module prevents users from sending the quotation by email or confirming the sales order. This ensures that quotations with low margins or higher business risk cannot be finalized without proper authorization. Additionally, the entire approval mechanism can be enabled or disabled through the company settings, allowing organizations to adopt or bypass the approval process without affecting the standard Sales workflow.

01 After installing the module navigate to Settings > Sales, the Approval Matrix section contains a single checkbox — "Sales Approval Matrix" — that enables or disables the entire feature for the company. When enabled, quotations are checked against the Revenue/Margin Approval Matrix and require manager approval when they fall outside the safe range. When this checkbox is unchecked, quotations behave exactly as if the module were not installed — no zone is calculated, no banners are shown, and Send/Confirm are never blocked.

The approval rules are configured based on this matrix, and during module installation, all approval matrix records are automatically created according to the combinations shown in the chart. This serves as the default approval logic for the Sales Approval workflow.

03 From Sales > Configuration > Approval Matrix this view lists all Approval Matrix records available in the module. Each record specifies the revenue range, margin percentage range, and the corresponding approval zone (Red, Yellow, or Green). Users can add/update approval matrix rules whenever required.


A final configuration is required for the **Sales/Approval Managers** who will receive approval requests when a salesperson's margin falls below the defined limit. You can configure one or multiple managers here.

As a result, a "Manager Review Required" banner is displayed at the top of the quotation, indicating that managerial approval must be obtained before the quotation can be sent to the customer or confirmed. The Untaxed Amount and Margin values shown at the bottom of the quotation are used to determine the applicable approval zone. The approval status on the right is "Not Requested," indicating that the approval request has not yet been initiated.

05 The Approval Matrix tab provides a consolidated view of the approval details for the quotation. The Calculated Result section displays the automatically determined Approval Zone (Red) and the Overall Margin % (9.62%), based on the quotation values. The Approval Record section shows the current Approval Status as Not Requested, indicating that the approval process has not yet been initiated. You cannot send or confirm the quotation until it has been approved by the Sales Manager.

06 If the quotation is sent before the required approval is obtained, the system displays an error message indicating that an approval request must be submitted first.

07 The salesperson clicks Request Approval to initiate the approval process. Once the request is submitted, an activity notification is automatically assigned to the sales manager, prompting them to review the quotation and either approve or reject the request.

08 After clicking Request Approval, the approval status changes to Pending Approval. An activity is automatically assigned to the sales manager, containing the quotation's Revenue, Margin, and Approval Zone details for review. At the same time, the Approve and Reject buttons become available to the sales manager, enabling them to review and take action on the approval request immediately.

09 This dialog allows the Sales Manager to reject a quotation by providing a mandatory rejection reason. Once confirmed, the rejection reason is recorded in the quotation's history,

10 The sales manager reviews the quotation, rejects the approval request, and provides the reason "Margin is too Low." The rejection reason, along with the name of the user who rejected the request and the date and time of the action, is automatically recorded and displayed in the Approval Record section for future reference.

11 The salesperson updates the cost of one of the product lines, increasing the quotation's overall margin to 20%. The Untaxed Amount, Margin, and approval calculation are updated automatically, causing the quotation to move from the Red approval zone to the Yellow approval zone based on the configured Approval Matrix. After updating the quotation, the salesperson clicks Request Approval again to initiate a new approval request. Quotations that fall within the Green approval zone do not require approval and can be sent to the customer or confirmed directly without going through the approval process.

12 After the salesperson submits the updated quotation by clicking Request Approval again, the Approval Status changes back to Pending Approval. 13 The sales manager approves the quotation after reviewing the request. Once approved, the Approval Status changes to Approved, and the Send and Confirm buttons become available, allowing the salesperson to proceed with sending the quotation to the customer or confirming the sales order.

What is the purpose of the Sales Approval Matrix module?

The Sales Approval Matrix module automatically determines whether a sales quotation requires approval based on its Revenue and Overall Margin %. It categorizes quotations into Red, Yellow, or Green approval zones according to the configured Approval Matrix.

How is the approval zone calculated?

The approval zone is calculated using the quotation's Untaxed Amount (Revenue) and Overall Margin %. The system compares these values against the configured Approval Matrix and automatically assigns the appropriate approval zone.

Does the module create approval rules automatically?

Yes. When the module is installed, it automatically creates all default Approval Matrix records based on the predefined approval chart. These records can be modified or extended later according to business requirements.

Can new approval rules be added?

Yes. Sales Manager can create, edit, or delete Approval Matrix records at any time to match their organization's approval policies.

Which approval zones require approval?

Only quotations that fall into the Red or Yellow approval zones require approval before they can be sent to the customer or confirmed.

What happens if a quotation falls into the Green zone?

Quotations in the Green approval zone do not require approval. They can be sent to the customer and confirmed directly using the standard Odoo Sales workflow.

What happens if a user tries to send a quotation before approval?

The system prevents the action and displays a validation message instructing the user to request approval before the quotation can be sent or confirmed.

Can the Sales Manager provide a reason when rejecting a quotation?

Yes. When rejecting a request, the sales manager can enter a rejection reason, which is stored along with the reviewer, date, and time in the Approval Record.

What happens if the quotation is modified after being rejected?

If the quotation is updated and its revenue or margin changes, the approval zone is recalculated automatically. The salesperson can then submit a new approval request based on the updated quotation.
