How does AI identify low stock products?

AI compares available stock, incoming quantities, reserved stock, historical sales movement, and forecast demand to determine whether replenishment is required.

Which AI models are supported in this module?

The module supports a wide range of the latest AI models from OpenAI, Anthropic Claude, and Google Gemini, allowing you to choose the model that best fits your business requirements.

Can the module create RFQs automatically?

Yes. When the Auto Create RFQ option is enabled, AI can automatically prepare vendor-wise RFQ recommendations based on inventory analysis.

Does this module support forecast-based analysis?

Yes. You can configure sales forecast days and purchase forecast days to allow AI to analyze only selected periods or complete historical data.

How does vendor recommendation work?

AI compares vendor delivery performance, lead times, purchase reliability, historical delays, and pricing information to recommend the most suitable vendor.

How do I track how many API tokens are being used?

Every AI log record shows the Total Tokens Used for that particular run. You can refer to this figure to monitor your API consumption and plan your provider usage accordingly.
