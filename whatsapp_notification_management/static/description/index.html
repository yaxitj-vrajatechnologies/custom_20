WhatsApp Notification Management allows businesses to automatically send important updates and reminders to customers through WhatsApp, helping maintain consistent and effective communication. With this feature, businesses can notify customers about abandoned carts, encouraging them to complete their purchase if they leave items in their cart without checking out. It also enables sending offer notifications to promote special deals and seasonal discounts to selected customers.

In addition, businesses can announce new product launches to keep customers informed about newly available products. The system also supports discount coupon alerts, allowing companies to notify customers about available offers and promotional codes. For selected customers, businesses can send personalized pricing updates for specific products, providing tailored offers. Furthermore, the feature helps manage payments by sending overdue invoice reminders, requesting customers to clear pending invoices. Overall, this automated notification system improves customer engagement, promotes offers effectively, and ensures timely follow-ups with customers.

01 Whatsapp Notification Management Configuration Process

02 Configure and connect your WhatsApp Business Account in Odoo to enable seamless sending of automated WhatsApp notifications to customers from Configuration > WhatsApp Business Accounts.

03 Enter the required WhatsApp API credentials (App ID, App Secret, Phone Number ID, WhatsApp Business Account ID, Access Token) to securely connect Odoo with your WhatsApp Business account and enable automated messaging. After entering the details, simply click Test Credentials to verify the connection.

04 The module comes preloaded with all default WhatsApp notification templates, so once installed, users can immediately use them without creating templates manually.

05 Select the configured WhatsApp Business account and submit the template for approval directly from Odoo to ensure it’s ready for sending notifications to customers.

06 Once a template is submitted for approval, it enters a pending state. Meta verifies and approves the template, after which it moves to the approved state. Once approved, you can send WhatsApp notifications to customers using the template and track its usage directly in Odoo.

How do I connect my WhatsApp Business account with Odoo?

You can connect your WhatsApp Business account from Configuration → WhatsApp Settings by entering the required API credentials such as App ID, App Secret, Phone Number ID, WhatsApp Business Account ID, and Access Token, and then clicking Test Credentials to verify the connection.

Do I need to create WhatsApp templates manually after installing the module?

No. The module provides pre-configured default templates for different notifications such as offers, product launches, coupons, abandoned carts, and overdue invoices. You only need to submit them for approval.

Why do WhatsApp templates need approval before sending messages?

WhatsApp (Meta) requires template approval to ensure that messages follow their messaging policies. Once the template is approved, it can be used to send notifications to customers.

Can I schedule WhatsApp notifications automatically?

Yes. Notifications are automatically triggered using Odoo cron jobs (Scheduled Actions), ensuring messages are sent at the configured time without manual intervention.

Can I send WhatsApp notifications manually?

Yes. In addition to automated notifications, you can manually send notifications by clicking the Send Notification button from the relevant record.

How can I target specific customers for notifications?

You can use customer tags to target specific groups of customers. Only customers with the selected tags will receive the WhatsApp notification.

Can I control which products an offer applies to?

Yes. Offers can be applied to All Products, Included Products, or Excluded Products, giving you flexibility to control which products are part of the promotion.

Can I modify the WhatsApp template body by default?

Yes, you can modify the default WhatsApp template body in Odoo before submitting it for approval. However, once the template is submitted and approved by Meta (WhatsApp), any changes to the template content will require re-submission and approval again before it can be used for sending notifications.
