Import Operations

1) Import Customers with essential details such as name, email, phone number, billing addresses.

2) Import Chart of Accounts and map QuickBooks accounts with Odoo accounts seamlessly.

3) Import Taxes from QuickBooks and automatically configure them in Odoo.

4) Import Payment Terms and synchronize them with Odoo customer and vendor records.

5) Import Products with key information including SKU, description, sales price, cost, and category.

Export Operations

1) Export Customers created or updated in Odoo directly to QuickBooks.

2) Export Products along with name, SKU, and pricing details.

3) Export Invoices and Bills from Odoo to QuickBooks for accurate financial synchronization.

4) Export Customer Payments and Vendor Payments to keep QuickBooks ledgers up to date.

5) Automatically schedule Invoice, Bill, Payment, and Vendor Payment synchronization at regular intervals.

01 Instance creation and configuration process for QuickBooks connection.

02 Configure your QuickBooks instance in Odoo this menu allows you to create and manage new instances easily.

03 In the instance configuration, enter your QuickBooks credentials to establish a secure connection between Odoo and QuickBooks.

04 From your QuickBooks Workspace, click 'Get Production Key' to retrieve the client ID and client secret required to connect your QuickBooks account with Odoo.

05 Click 'Redirect URL' in your QuickBooks app settings to specify the redirect URL.

06 Copy this URL and configure it in Odoo to establish a secure connection between the two platforms.

07 When you click 'Connect', Odoo will redirect you to the QuickBooks authorization screen to establish a connection with your company account.

08 Choose your QuickBooks company and click 'Next'.

09 You’ll be redirected to Odoo, and the instance will automatically establish a successful connection.

10 In the import configuration, when the options for Customer, Chart of Accounts, Payment Terms, Products, and Taxes are enabled, the data from QuickBooks will be automatically created in Odoo during the import process.

11 In the Export Configuration, enable Send Invoice Tax Included to ensure taxes are included when exporting invoices to QuickBooks.

You can also enable Automatic Invoice/Bill Export to QuickBooks and Automatic Payment Export to QuickBooks to schedule automatic synchronization.

This feature exports invoices, bills, and payments from Odoo to QuickBooks automatically based on the defined export date, ensuring your financial data stays up to date.

Auto Update Invoice/Bill: Automatically updates any changes made to exported invoices and bills in QuickBooks, keeping both systems perfectly aligned.

Auto Update Payments: Automatically updates to customer and vendor payments that were already exported, ensuring real-time accuracy of payment records across Odoo and QuickBooks.

Does the application support both QuickBooks Online and QuickBooks Desktop?

This application is designed specifically for QuickBooks Online. QuickBooks Desktop is not supported in this version.

Can I manage multiple QuickBooks instances (companies)?

Yes, you can manage and sync multiple QuickBooks Online instances from Odoo. Each instance can be configured with its own credentials, settings, and synchronization preferences.

What type of data can I import from QuickBooks to Odoo?

You can import the following records from QuickBooks to Odoo:

1. Customers 2. Payment Terms 3. Taxes 4. Chart of Accounts 5. Products

During import, you’ll have an option to create records in Odoo if they don’t already exist or skip them as per your configuration.

What type of data can I export from Odoo to QuickBooks?

You can export the following data from Odoo to QuickBooks:

1. Customers 2. Vendors 3. Products 4. Customer Invoices / Vendor Bills 5. Customer Payments / Vendor Payments

Does the integration handle updates for invoices or payments?

Yes. Any updates made to Invoices / Vendor Bills or Payments in Odoo can be synced to QuickBooks to keep both systems in sync.

Are there scheduled actions (cron jobs) for automatic synchronization?

Yes.

The app includes three automatic scheduled actions (cron jobs):

1. Token Refresh Cron – Automatically refreshes QuickBooks API tokens to maintain authentication. 2. Invoice/Bill Export Cron – Automatically exports Odoo invoices and vendor bills based on the instance’s date filter. 3. Payment Export Cron – Automatically exports Odoo customer/vendor payments using the instance’s date range.

You can also run these operations manually at any time.

Can I control what gets created automatically in Odoo?

Yes. During import, you can choose whether to automatically create missing records (like customers, products, etc.) or skip them if not found. This option can be configured per instance.

How are taxes and chart of accounts handled?

1. Taxes and chart of accounts are imported directly from QuickBooks. 2. The app ensures correct linking of tax codes and accounts between Odoo and QuickBooks to maintain financial consistency.
